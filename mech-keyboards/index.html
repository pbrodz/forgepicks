The Short List

Six true splits and three Alice-layout boards. Every one is programmable (QMK, ZMK, VIA, or equivalent), because a keyboard you can't remap is a paperweight. Prices and specs change — verify before you buy.

Our pick

ZSA Moonlander (~$365) — the split board we’d hand to anyone who types all day: columnar stagger, a real thumb cluster, and programming that doesn’t require learning C. See why ↓

Prefer a one-piece board: FEKER Alice98 (~$100) — our top Alice pick: angled ergonomic layout with none of the split learning curve. See why ↓
