About This Site

KEYHOLD is a small experiment: can a focused, honest site about ergonomic mechanical keyboards earn its traffic on merit? No sponsored placements, no fake "9.7/10" review scores, no content farm. Just picks we'd defend in an argument.

The short version of the philosophy:

  • Programmability is non-negotiable. If a programmer can't remap it, it's not on the list.
  • Comfort is personal. Hands differ; we describe trade-offs instead of declaring one true board.
  • Prices and specs are checked at write time and marked with a "last updated" date. Verify before buying — the market moves.

If a pick stops deserving its spot, it gets replaced. That's the deal.
